## Astronot görselleri

- Dosya: `astronaut-lineup.png`.
- Yerleşim: soldan sağa üç görünüş; CSS ile her panel ayrı gösterilir.
- Yerleşik ImageGen aracıyla üretildi. Bunlar seçim ekranı görselleridir; oynanabilir, iskeletli karakter modelleri değildir.
- Ülke, milliyet, bayrak veya farklı yetenek etiketi kullanılmaz.

Kullanılan üretim istemi:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Production asset for a photorealistic space exploration video game character selection, NOT a UI mockup. Make one wide 3:2 image divided into THREE equal-width vertical portrait panels, separated by subtle dark empty gutters. Each panel contains one DIFFERENT adult male astronaut from head to knees, same straight standing relaxed three-quarter pose, helmet OFF, holding his helmet tucked under his left arm by his hip, face clearly visible, hands anatomically correct. Matching realistic ivory-white EVA suits with graphite articulated joints, restrained orange stitching, no flags, no nationality symbols, no logos, no written text. LEFT panel: dark-skinned Black man, close-cropped dark hair, clean shaven. CENTER panel: fair-skinned man, short blond hair, clearly blue eyes, clean shaven. RIGHT panel: lightly tanned olive complexion man, short dark hair and a neat dark mustache, no beard. All mature adult men around 30-40. Consistent suit and pose across the three. Each character centered WITHIN HIS OWN THIRD of the image, generously spaced from panel edges. Moody dark spacecraft hangar background with subtle warm rim lighting, sharp high-end cinematic game realism, physically realistic cloth, photographic faces. Frame each man identically, with the helmet fully in-frame under arm. All three the same height. No screen graphics, no text, no watermark.
